# Vocabulaire

Vocabulaire is the focused vocabulary trainer for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Learn vocabulary faster, more effectively, and completely distraction-free.

### Learn like there's no tomorrow:

* With the FSRS spaced-repetition algorithm, every card is reviewed right before you'd otherwise forget it.
* Set a daily limit for new cards and stay in control of your learning workload.

### Listen to learn:

* Generate natural pronunciation via text-to-speech, or record your own.
* Train vocabulary and pronunciation at the same time.

### The professional way to learn a language:

* Built-in conjugation editor: store verb tenses for each word and learn them as their own cards.
* Create classic flashcard sets for anything beyond languages.

### Whether on the go or at home:

* Sync vocabulary, pronunciations, and learning progress across iPhone, iPad, and Mac.
* Import and export entire vocabulary boxes, including audio files, to back up or share.

### Crystal clear, zero distractions:

* Clean, beautiful design.
* Full control over groups, boxes, and individual vocabulary items.

**Create unlimited vocabulary, groups, and boxes locally and learn offline — completely free.**
