## 2. Overview of Processing

Vocabulaire is an iOS and macOS app for learning vocabulary. To provide its features, the following services and data
are processed:

| Purpose                           | Service                                       | Data processed                                                                |
|-----------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Sign-in / account                 | Firebase Authentication (Google)              | Email address, user ID                                                        |
| Syncing vocabulary across devices | Cloud Firestore (Google)                      | Vocabulary lists, learning progress (FSRS values)                             |
| Storing audio recordings          | Firebase Storage (Google)                     | Pronunciation recordings you create                                           |
| Text-to-speech                    | Cloud Functions + Google Cloud Text-to-Speech | Text submitted for speech synthesis                                           |
| Abuse/bot protection              | Firebase App Check (Google)                   | Device/app integrity data                                                     |
| Subscriptions / in-app purchases  | Apple In-App Purchase                         | Purchase receipt, subscription status (processed by Apple)                    |
| App crash analytics               | Firebase Crashlytics (Google)                 | Application status at crash (stack trace) and actions that lead to the crash. |

## 3. Legal Basis

Processing is carried out to fulfil the usage contract (Art. 6 (1)(b) GDPR) and, where applicable, on the basis of our
legitimate interest in a functioning, secure app (Art. 6 (1)(f) GDPR).

## 4. Recipients and Third Parties

The services listed above are provided by Google (Firebase/Google Cloud) and Apple, either as processors or as
independent controllers. Their own privacy policies also apply:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)

## 5. Retention

Data is retained for as long as your account exists. After account deletion, your vocabulary data and audio recordings
are deleted, unless legal retention obligations require otherwise.

## 6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict processing of, and port your personal data, as well as to object
to its processing. Contact us via the email address above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data
protection supervisory authority.
