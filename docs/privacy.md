# Privacy Policy

Vocabulaire collects and processes personal data in order to provide its features. This privacy policy explains what
data that is and how it is processed.

Gregor Gottschewski

## 2. Overview of Processing

Vocabulaire is an iOS and macOS app for learning vocabulary. To provide its features, the following services and data
are processed:

| Purpose                           | Service                                       | Data processed                                                                |
|------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Sign-in / account                 | Firebase Authentication (Google)              | Email address, user ID                                                        |
| Syncing vocabulary across devices | Cloud Firestore (Google)                      | Vocabulary lists, learning progress (FSRS values)                             |
| Storing audio recordings          | Firebase Storage (Google)                     | Pronunciation recordings you create                                           |
| Text-to-speech                    | Cloud Functions + Google Cloud Text-to-Speech | Text submitted for speech synthesis                                           |
| Abuse/bot protection              | Firebase App Check (Google)                   | Device/app integrity data, device attestation token                           |
| Subscriptions / in-app purchases  | Apple In-App Purchase                         | Purchase receipt, subscription status (processed by Apple)                    |
| App crash analytics               | Firebase Crashlytics (Google)                 | Application status at crash (stack trace) and actions that lead to the crash. |

## 3. Legal Basis

Processing is carried out to fulfil the usage contract (Art. 6 (1)(b) GDPR) and, where applicable, on the basis of our
legitimate interest in a functioning, secure app (Art. 6 (1)(f) GDPR).

## 4. Recipients and Third Parties

The services listed above are provided by Google (Firebase/Google Cloud) and Apple, either as processors or as
independent controllers. Their own privacy policies also apply:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)

## 5. International Data Transfers

Our Cloud Functions run in the EU (region `europe-west1`). However, as part of their global infrastructure, Google
and Apple may also transfer personal data to countries outside the EU/EEA, in particular the United States. Such
transfers are based on adequacy decisions (including the EU-US Data Privacy Framework) or EU Standard Contractual
Clauses pursuant to Art. 46 GDPR.

## 6. Retention

Data is retained for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account in the app, your vocabulary data,
audio recordings, and related usage data are automatically deleted server-side, unless legal retention obligations
require otherwise.

## 7. Automated Decision-Making

No automated decision-making, including profiling, within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR takes place.

## 8. Children

Vocabulaire is not directed at children. Individuals under the age of 16 require the consent of a parent or legal
guardian to use the app and the associated data processing (Art. 8 GDPR).

## 9. Your Rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict processing of, and port your personal data, as well as to object
to its processing. Contact us via the email address above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data
protection supervisory authority.

## 10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We update this privacy policy whenever the app or the services it uses change. The current version is always
available via the app and this website; the "last updated" date below is authoritative.
